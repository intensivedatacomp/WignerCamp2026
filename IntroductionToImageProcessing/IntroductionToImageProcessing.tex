\section{Our Method}


\begin{frame}{Convolution Based Approach}


    Images can be processed using convolution kernels.


    \[
        \begin{bmatrix}
            -1 & -1 & -1 \\
            0  & 0  & 0  \\
            1  & 1  & 1
        \end{bmatrix}
    \]


    The kernel scans the image and detects patterns.


\end{frame}
